GP Fieldnotes

A quiet, searchable study space for evidence and argument. Explore global and Singapore cases, unpack the reasoning, and build AQ responses from local examples. Each evidence card links to a source so you can verify the detail before memorising it.

Evidence standard: For each example, memorise both the qualitative mechanism (what happened and why it matters) and a quantitative anchor (a dated statistic, scale, cost or outcome) wherever one is available. The cards flag examples whose evidence text currently has no number; for those, pair the case with a verified statistic rather than inventing one. Figures may describe allegations, survey intentions or a specific sample—not necessarily proven causal effects.

Environment

How to use this bank: For each example, learn (1) the specific evidence, (2) what it proves, (3) how it answers the question, and (4) the limitation/counterargument. “Singapore traits” are possible analytical lenses—not labels that automatically explain every Singaporean. Select only the ones you can justify in your essay. Figures are dated and source-linked where available. Official statistics and primary policy pages are preferred; news/context sources are supplementary. Distinguish announced plans from implemented outcomes, and verify the year, population definition and source before memorising a statistic.
